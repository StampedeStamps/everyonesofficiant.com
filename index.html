About me

I'm Douglas Boynton. I became an ordained minister in 2008, and I've had the joy of officiating ceremonies since 2017. I'm a non-denominational wedding officiant, and I'm LGBTQ+ friendly. Whoever you are and whatever you believe, my job is to make your day feel like yours.

A wedding day can bring a lot of stress. I strive to be a calming, reassuring presence that eases it. I look forward to being a part of what is arguably the most important day in your life!
